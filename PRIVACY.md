# Yard Puck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Yard Puck is a sports scores app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Yard Puck does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Your favorite teams, the leagues you follow, and your settings (appearance, haptics, and score alerts) are saved only on your iPhone and, if you use it, your Apple Watch. They are shared between the app, its widgets, and the Watch app on your own devices. They are never sent to us.

## Sports data

To show scores, standings, schedules, news, and stats, the app requests public sports data directly from ESPN and other public stats sources, such as the NHL. Like any web request, these services can see your device's IP address and basic request details. Yard Puck sends them no personal information. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

## Notifications

Score alerts and Live Activities are created on your device from the public sports data above. If you allow notifications, they are delivered locally on your device.

## Children

Yard Puck does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
